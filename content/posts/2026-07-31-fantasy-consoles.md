---
title: Why I Built a Fantasy Console
date: 2026-07-30
---

Why build [Lazy-100](https://github.com/zzxzzk115/Lazy-100) when PICO-8 and
TIC-80 are right there? The honest answer has two halves.

The practical half: Lazy-100 exercises
[VRI](https://github.com/zzxzzk115/VRI), my cross-backend render hardware
interface, as a complete product rather than a triangle demo — 320×240
indexed color, sprite and sound editors, a Lua VM, running on desktop and
in the browser. If the abstraction holds there, it holds.

The honest half: I wanted one. Constraints are the fun part. A 256-color
palette and 16×16 sprites is not a limitation — it's a permission slip to
stop worrying about art quality and start making games.

(It also runs on this site, in the Games folder.)
